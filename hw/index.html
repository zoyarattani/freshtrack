About FreshTrack

FreshTrack is an idea for an app that helps people remember what groceries they have at home and when those groceries expire.

Sometimes food gets pushed to the back of the fridge or pantry and is forgotten. FreshTrack would make it easier to see what food you already have before it goes bad.

The goal is to help people save money, stay organized, and reduce food waste.

Features

Track Food

Users could scan grocery barcodes or add food manually.

Expiration Reminders

FreshTrack could send a reminder when an item is getting close to its expiration date.

Organize Groceries

Items could be separated into categories so users can easily see what they have.

How It Works

A user would add groceries to FreshTrack after shopping.

  1. Open FreshTrack.
  2. Add a grocery item.
  3. Enter its expiration date.
  4. Save the item.
  5. Check which foods need to be used soon.
  6. Remove items after using them.

Why Use FreshTrack?

Reduce Food Waste

Reminders could help people use groceries before they expire.

Save Money

Using food before it goes bad means fewer groceries have to be thrown away.

Stay Organized

Users could check one list instead of trying to remember everything in their refrigerator and pantry.
