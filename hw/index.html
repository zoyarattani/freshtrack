About FreshTrack

FreshTrack is an app that helps people keep track of the groceries they have at home and reminds them before something expires.

The idea behind FreshTrack is to make it easier for people to remember what food they already have and avoid throwing away groceries that were forgotten in the fridge or pantry.

The main goal of FreshTrack is to help people save money, stay organized, and reduce food waste.

Features

FreshTrack would include several features to make keeping track of groceries easier.

Track Food

Scan grocery barcodes or manually add food items to your account.

Get Reminders

Receive reminders when food is getting close to its expiration date.

Stay Organized

Organize groceries into categories and see which foods should be used first.

One of the most useful features would be the expiration reminder, which would alert users before food goes bad.

How It Works

Using FreshTrack would only take a few simple steps.

  1. Open the FreshTrack app.
  2. Scan the barcode of a grocery item or add it manually.
  3. Enter the expiration date.
  4. Save the item to your grocery list.
  5. Receive a reminder when the food should be used soon.
  6. Remove the item after it has been used.

Why FreshTrack?

Reduce Food Waste

FreshTrack could remind users to use food before it expires instead of forgetting about it in the refrigerator or pantry.

Save Money

Throwing away unused groceries also means wasting money. Keeping track of expiration dates could help people use more of the food they buy.

Stay Organized

FreshTrack would give users one place to see what groceries they have and which items should be used first.
